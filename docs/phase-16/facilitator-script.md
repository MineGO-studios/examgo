# Alpha facilitator script and task cards

ExamGO by MineGO Studio · protocol v1 · materials only · 19 October 2026.

Copy this sheet into approved private session storage before use. All blank fields mean not yet recorded. Do not save completed sheets in the repository or shared brain.

## Current delivery mode — 21 October 2026

The owner is the single coordinator and support contact and can communicate with teachers through text, without direct observation. Use the short [private text pack](teacher-text-pack.md): one recent-work question, two task cards sent separately and three debrief questions. Keep technical recording out of participant messages. The original observed-session script below remains available for an observation-capable session; it does not imply that an observer is currently available.

Text replies about time, actions or help are self-reported. Inspect returned document pairs separately. Use the protocol's dated method and retention clarifications; no teacher participation or independence gate is passed by preparing materials or receiving an unverified completion message. Feedback/received-copy retention is 30 days after the trial ends, manually handled by the owner. Confirm the actual end-date and private-route arrangements before use. A text-round rehearsal can check message clarity and sequencing, but is not teacher usability evidence.

## Welcome and consent — read before observing

“We are trying an early private version of ExamGO by MineGO Studio to understand how it works for Grade 6 English teachers. We are testing the product, not your teaching or computer skills. Participation is voluntary. You may pause, skip a question or stop at any time. Please use only the supplied private account and material you are allowed to use; do not enter student names, completed student answers or other personal information.

“With your agreement, I will take notes about actions, time, help needed and document problems under a participant code. Contact details and account access stay separate. We will not record your screen or voice unless we ask separately and you agree. Please do not show passwords or private messages. We may retain the exact paper and answer-key pair privately to investigate problems, with your permission. Tell us if you want an identifiable note or artifact removed; the owner will handle that request and explain any limits before we continue.

“This is an alpha, so check all answers, marks and document layout before using a paper. Browser preview does not guarantee Word page fit. The support contact provided to you handles problems; no response-time promise applies. Are you comfortable proceeding with notes? May we keep the generated pair privately for review?”

Record the answers privately. If consent is missing, stop observation. If artifact retention is declined, agree on a privacy-respecting review route and mark any unavailable gate evidence; do not pressure the teacher. This session wording is not Phase 17 legal/privacy-policy approval.

Before asking for consent, read the actual owner-specified retention period and deletion contact from the readiness record. If these remain undefined, do not begin the session or invent a promise.

## Understand the recent real task

Ask before demonstrating features:

1. “Tell me about the last Grade 6 English paper you prepared. Can you show a redacted source or describe it?”
2. “Where did its questions and answers come from? What did you need to check or change?”
3. “Show how you normally find a question, assemble the paper, check answers and finish in Word.”
4. “Which parts take effort or cause mistakes? What happens when a question, file or answer is missing?”
5. “Which device, language/direction and Word setup do you actually use?”

Record observed role/needs and current method without guessing age or job distribution. If baseline work cannot be observed, label it self-reported. Agree on the two realistic briefs before timing; record any unsupported need as a gap. Do not tell the teacher that ExamGO is faster or easier.

## Standard orientation

Provide only the private link, invited account instructions, data/consent limits and the task goal. Let the teacher sign in privately. Separate orientation and bank/import setup timing from composition. Explain that both student paper and answer key must be checked and that Word editing is part of the task. Do not demonstrate the sequence being measured. If the teacher requests help after an attempt starts, offer help without pressure and record the intervention.

## Task card A — give to the teacher

“Using the supplied private material, prepare the short classroom paper described in brief A. Find a question you would use and make the agreed change. Check the questions, answers and marks. Save it, find it again, and download the student paper and answer key. Open both in Word and tell me whether you could use them, and what you would correct.”

## Task card B — give to the teacher

“Review the supplied question CSV and add the suitable questions to your separate practice bank only when you are satisfied with the review. Then use the ready private material for the different paper described in brief B. Check all instructions, linked context, answers and marks, save it, and review the student paper and answer key. Open both in Word and tell me what you would correct before using them.”

Observe CSV recognition, any column choices, preview, answers/context and the teacher's final confirmation in a separately timed preparation segment. The optional two-row CSV is synthetic practice only; use the pre-reviewed bank for the real paper. Do not switch to unsupported PDF/Word/OCR intake. If the brief cannot be met with available tools/content, record a block instead of substituting an easier task.

## Observer prompts and checks — do not read as coaching

Use neutral prompts: “What are you looking for?”, “What do you expect next?”, “What makes you trust that answer?” Wait for the teacher to act. Do not name hidden controls, mapping internals, seeds or snapshot terminology to rescue the task.

Record search/edit/import comprehension; missing-context and wrong-answer detection; student/key switching; total/point marks and attempt rules; pagination warnings; draft/saved/final distinctions; reopen behavior; errors/retry and paired download recovery. Do not inject wrong answers, force sign-out, disconnect the network or create destructive failures during an ordinary teacher session. Observe natural problems; engineering rehearsals remain a separate layer.

Record timestamps, pauses, interventions, abandonment reason and corrections. Confirm exact pair identity/hash and human Word observations. Use [protocol completion rules](protocol.md), not observer enthusiasm, to classify the attempt.

## Debrief

- “What surprised or confused you? Show the part you mean.”
- “What would stop you trusting or using this pair?”
- “Compared with your usual method, what was easier or harder?”
- “What did you have to correct in ExamGO or Word?”
- “When, if ever, would you choose this for another paper? Why?”
- “Anything you avoided or could not finish?”

Record intention separately from actual repeat use. Close by confirming the private support route and any permission to retain/redact the pair. Summarize defects without promising a feature, deadline or successful alpha outcome.
