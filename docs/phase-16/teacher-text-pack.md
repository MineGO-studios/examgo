# Private teacher trial by text

ExamGO by MineGO Studio · preparation material · `1.0.0-alpha.1` documentation checkpoint · 21 October 2026.

For the owner: use your existing private conversation with each teacher. Send one task at a time. Fill the approved Preview link, private account instructions, two feasible paper briefs and trial end-date arrangements before sending. You are the deletion contact in that conversation. The owner selected 30 days after the trial ends for collected feedback and paper copies; record the actual end date and manual deletion deadline privately. Do not send this preparation paragraph. No teacher has completed these tasks merely because the messages exist.

## Message 1 — introduction and permission

“I’m preparing an early private version of ExamGO for Grade 6 English teachers. Could you try two paper-making tasks and tell me how they go? This tests the product, not your skills. You can pause or stop whenever you want.

“Please use only material you are allowed to use. Don’t include student names, student answers, passwords or private messages. We’ll use this private conversation for help. Please check all answers, marks and Word layout before using a paper in class.

“With your permission, I’ll keep your feedback and the student paper/key copies you send me privately until 30 days after the trial ends. I’ll confirm the end date with you here. Contact me here to ask for earlier removal; I’ll explain any limits before we start. This does not delete papers or banks in your ExamGO account. There is no screen or voice recording. Are you comfortable taking part and sharing those files for review?”

Wait for an answer. If consent or the practical private-storage/deletion route is unresolved, keep the invitation pending. If files cannot be shared, agree a safe review route and record the resulting evidence limit privately.

## Message 2 — one recent-work question

“What was the last Grade 6 English paper you prepared? Describe its question types, units and how you finished it in Word. You don’t need to send any student information.”

Use the answer to confirm two genuinely useful, different supported briefs. Do not substitute a simpler paper silently. Confirm the teacher can use Word and receive both downloads before assigning either task.

## Task card A — short assessment

“Using [private Preview/account instructions] and [agreed brief A], find a question you would use and make the agreed change in your private bank. Prepare the short assessment, check its answers and marks, save it, then find it again. Download the student paper and answer key. Open both in Word and tell me what you would correct before using them.”

## Task card B — different structure

“Using [agreed brief B] and the supplied reviewed material, prepare the different paper or complete exercise we agreed. Check the full instructions, question choices, answers and marks. Save and reopen it where this format supports that. Download the student paper and answer key, open both in Word, and tell me what you would correct.”

Brief B must differ in structure, not only title or question order. Supported CSV practice can be sent as a separate setup task; if postponed, record import as untested. Do not send both task cards together or direct the teacher to a hidden development tool.

## Reply after each task

“Please reply: completed, partly completed or stopped; approximately how long it took; where you needed help or changed the paper; and whether both Word files opened and were usable. If you agree and the files contain no personal information, send the original student paper and answer key together. Describe a problem before sending a screenshot or extra source files.”

A reported time is an estimate. A help-free claim is self-reported. A file review can verify that exact file pair, but it cannot establish which actions were performed independently.

## Three debrief questions

1. “What was hardest?”
2. “What must change before you would use this again?”
3. “Does the resulting paper fit your real work? What would you change?”

## Owner recording — keep out of participant messages

You are the single coordinator and support contact. No additional staff are assumed. Keep real replies, consent, account details and files in owner-controlled private storage. The repository provides only a blank [text-feedback record](records/text-feedback.csv).

For the collected trial feedback, notes and received paper copies, manually remove owner-held copies 30 days after the recorded trial end date. Record the deadline and completion privately. Handle earlier removal requests through the same private conversation. This kit creates no automatic deletion or reminder and does not change application-bank, saved-paper, account or operational-log retention. Do not put participant data or actual deletion records into this repository or the shared Brain.

Label reported actions, estimated times, reported help and intended reuse as self-reported. Record independently inspected file facts separately, with the original pair hashes and reviewer/Word details. A corrected copy is a new artifact. Link each later retest to the original report rather than replacing it.

Use [support and triage](support-and-triage.md) for defects. Stop the affected task on suspected exposure, data loss, wrong answers or invalid output. Do not coach a teacher into a passing result or promise a fix date.

Text-only feedback does not satisfy the existing directly observed-workflow and independent-success evidence requirements by itself. Preserve the [protocol](protocol.md) gates; record this round as remote text feedback with observation unavailable. Any later change to formal acceptance needs an explicit owner decision recorded before applying it.
