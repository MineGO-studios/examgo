# Prepare a paper

Applies to: `1.0.0-alpha.1`

Last reviewed: 21 October 2026

ExamGO is an Alpha workspace for Iraqi Grade 6 English paper preparation. Access, available questions and paper formats depend on the account and build.

## Before starting

Use an authorized account and content you are permitted to use. Know the required units, question forms, displayed and attempted counts, marks, and intended student-paper length. Have a way to inspect the final Word files.

## Choose content

Open **Question banks**. **Active bank** includes your personal banks and shared official banks marked **(Preset)**. Select either kind in the same control. Beside the combined preset bank, Unit 1 (Preset), Unit 2 (Preset), Unit 3 (Preset) and Unit 4 (Preset) open separate views of the corresponding unit. These views share the published source; questions are not copied. The combined preset retains all four units. Linked passage context remains complete when an exercise spans units.

Both kinds use **Questions by unit**, search, type/context filters and the paged question list. Select a unit bar to narrow the list; **Show every unit** clears that filter. Unit totals describe the entire selected bank. The current published source covers Units 1–4, subject to current eligibility.

Personal banks retain Add question, Import file, Passages and Manage banks. Official preset banks are read-only: importing, source editing and bank management are unavailable. **Passages** shows published passage text and ordered question summaries with the recorded instructions. **Reload bank** refreshes the published content. **Create from approved presets** opens the existing supported paper workflows; browsing does not expand their unit or format limits.

Current and future signed-in accounts receive the same preset access. Personal banks remain private. A question can belong to several personal banks without becoming several copies; passages and linked questions retain their shared context.

Use the supported import workflow for a supported file, confirm unfamiliar column mappings, and check questions and answers before accepting conversions. Unresolved rows remain unresolved; importing a file does not automatically approve or publish preset content.

Approved-preset availability is narrower than total imported inventory. If a required type, unit or complete group is unavailable, change the request deliberately or ask for reviewed content.

The current hosted paper workflows support Unit 1. Unit 2–4 bank browsing does not enable those units for generation. The dated [coverage reference](../reference/alpha-content-and-builder-coverage-2026-10-21.md) records usable supply; confirm it again for the selected build. A short personal-bank paper needs suitable private questions, while a complete approved exercise uses its entire published group and recorded attempt rule.

## Configure the paper

Open **New paper** and choose what kind of paper to make:

- **Pick questions by type** builds a paper from your banks with chosen counts of multiple-choice, true/false or yes/no, and short-answer questions. **Shared MineGO questions** appears here as a source. It shows as unavailable, with its reason, until approved standalone questions are published.
- **Use a preset exam paper made by MineGO** makes a ready-made MineGO paper, kept in source order with its answer key.

Iraqi exercises and writing is no longer offered as a new paper. Saved drafts of that kind still open from **My papers**.

A kind of paper you cannot make yet stays visible with a short reason, for example a teacher without any bank sees "Add or import questions to a bank in Question banks first".

Some structured tools are development-only. Do not assume a local development option is available in a hosted Alpha build.

Enter school/test details and an optional teacher footer name. Choose supported banks, filters, quantities and marks. Displayed items and attempted items may differ; verify the attempted total.

Papers fit on two pages, so there is no page choice. A short note appears if the chosen questions are estimated to need more than two pages.

**Selection number:** when more questions fit than the paper needs, your selection number decides which are picked. Each account has its own number, so your papers differ from other teachers' papers built with the same settings. Keep it to get the same questions again. You can change it in **Settings › Question selection**; it is kept on that device for your account.

## Review

Check wording, answers, numbering, complete passages/groups, options, instructions, marks and attempt rules. Use only the controls supported by the selected format. A locked item remains protected from supported replacement operations.

After a configuration or content change, review the updated selection. Do not use a stale preview as confirmation of the new settings. If no valid replacement exists, adjust the request explicitly; do not substitute unrelated content.

Page estimates are advisory. Do not assume browser pagination exactly matches Word.

## Settings

Open **Settings** to sign out, sign out of all devices, or change two choices kept on this device: the interface language, and whether My papers opens as Desk or List. Everyone using the same device shares those two choices. Light or dark appearance follows the device's own setting. Saved paper defaults, such as a school name, are not available yet.

In Arabic, New paper (both kinds of paper, setup, review, saving and downloads), Question banks (the question list, the question form, passages and file import), My papers and Settings are translated, along with sign-in, session and recovery messages, account limits and save failures. The paper, its answer key and your question content stay in the language you wrote them in. The MineGO reviewer tools are translated too; source records keep the language they were written in.

## Save and download

Save editable work when the selected workflow supports it. **My papers** distinguishes editable configurations from finalized papers. Finalized papers preserve captured content; later bank changes do not rewrite them. Simple-paper downloads do not create the same immutable finalized history as structured workflows.

Prepare and download the **Student paper** and **Answer key** from the same reviewed or finalized version. Check that both files arrived. A partial download is not a successful pair; retry the failed file through the supported action.

The [shared paper-format rules](../reference/paper-format-rules.md) place grade and school in their exchanged header positions and omit individual point marks from student papers. Section totals and answer-key scoring remain. These rules are implemented in the current uncommitted candidate; the older hosted Preview has not yet been updated. Confirm the exact build before expecting the revised output.

Open both documents in the required target application and check answers, marks, numbering, text direction and page breaks before classroom use. Report a problem with the product version, paper format, exact build and the smallest safe example. Do not include student data or account credentials.
