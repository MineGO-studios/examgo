MCQ answer labels resolve to supplied options; binary answers are boolean; short/rewritten/relationship answers exist; completion blank IDs have answers; matching pairs refer to supplied left/right IDs; writing has model/manual-marking guidance. These checks found no issues. All 52 visible passage groups have text.

Source marks are unknown on all 1,593 eligible records. This is preserved uncertainty, not a fabricated zero or automatic defect. Supported paper paths use explicit exam-owned marks; the complete-preset projection uses its requested per-point mark. Confirm actual displayed/attempted totals before assigning a brief.

Group context, source occurrence, curriculum intent and provenance may still differ. These are review candidates, not permission to merge/delete canonical records. No source answers, publication or content rows were changed.

Structural validity does not establish that every answer is educationally correct, that rights are adequate for a particular use, or that a teacher's intended paper is supported. Fresh educational/source review remains pending. Original source approvals retain their recorded scope.
